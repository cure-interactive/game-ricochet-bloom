AN INSTRUMENT WITH MOMENTUM

Play the pool.

Pull anywhere on the pool and release to fling the white seed in the opposite direction. A longer pull gives more power. Jelly bodies drift, transfer momentum, and bounce off the edges.

  • Touch a different jelly to add a note: 25 × its place in the chain. After a wall bank, each new note earns 25 extra.
  • Touch five unique jellies in one pluck for a 500-point bloom and an extra pluck. Each jelly counts once per pluck.
  • Make three blooms to win. Challenge mode starts with 12 plucks; blooms refund one. A flight starts with seven seconds; each collected power refreshes that countdown. A slowing seed can settle sooner. The flight finishes before the win or loss appears. Keep the same pool and song in free play afterward.
  • Free play has unlimited plucks, no loss, and separate scoring. Use Mode above the pool to resume either saved mode. New game replaces only the current mode; its Pool options let you change the seed.
  • Jelly powers activate on contact. Hit the marked jelly with your launched seed or either Split seed to activate it and refresh your flight to seven seconds. Ripple pushes nearby jellies away; Attract pulls them together; Vortex sends them swirling. Stasis slows nearby jellies; Comet speeds up your seed; Swap exchanges its position with the farthest other jelly. Start each pluck with one power opportunity. A successful pluck (a five-hit bloom) earns one more power opportunity for the next shot, up to three. A shot without a bloom resets the next shot to one. Hit each new marker to activate it; timed powers combine and expire independently. Miss the marker and that power stays available. Magnet draws unhit jellies toward your seed for 2.4 seconds; Lightning strikes and knocks back three jellies; Impact strengthens your pushes for the rest of the pluck. Open Powers below the pool to see their icons.
  • The markings identify ♪ melody, ≋ bass and ⋮ rhythm. Hit a part to reshape it as its layer unlocks. Choose Glass, Velvet or Wood for a different instrument character. Stronger hits add emphasis. The Bonus objective card shows a live target that awards 200 points; banked blooms and completed objectives earn an occasional fill, breathing space and return.
  • Use ← → to aim, ↑ ↓ to change power, Space to pluck, and P to pause.

Your exact moving pool saves every half-second, after launches, and at significant events. The song starts quietly. Plucks collect melodic ideas, and blooms add harmony and layers. Three blooms unlock the full song. After each shot, listen for a two-bar reply: the jellies light up with the notes you made. The first pluck touching three different jellies establishes a recurring melody. It repeats for eight bars, then new melody-jelly hits can change one half at the next section. The other half stays familiar. Shorter shots still play answering phrases; misses never erase your music. Bass and rhythm changes join at two-bar boundaries. Save favorites in Song library. New layers join on the next bar; the music continues between plucks, with quieter verses and fuller choruses. Adjust its own slider, or turn it down to hear only the jellies. Hiding the tab pauses it. Seeds reproduce the initial pool; timing your launch is part of the game. Scores stay local.
