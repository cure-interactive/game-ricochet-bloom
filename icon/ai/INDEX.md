The Basic icon accompanies a scoped disclosure. Its use does not imply European Commission endorsement, certification or legal compliance. The Fully AI-Generated and Partially AI-Modified variants are not used for the game.

## Scoped disclosure pills

The purple two-part HTML/CSS pills adapt the vocabulary and color scheme of [AI Disclosure Badges](https://disclosingai.com/) by [mau-vera](https://github.com/mau-vera/built-with-ai-badges), released under [CC0 1.0](https://github.com/mau-vera/built-with-ai-badges/blob/main/LICENSE). This is a community disclosure system, not a regulatory body. Local changes: accessible responsive HTML, the scopes Code and Selected Icons, and the tool name Codex.

Code and selected original SVG artwork have substantial generative AI contributions under Cure Interactive’s direction. Individual artwork pages retain their own authors, sources and licenses; imported artwork is not attributed to Codex. Runtime music and effects use programmed local systems, not a generative AI model.
