# powers.svg

[Artwork](powers.svg). An energized bubble with two smaller satellite bubbles
identifies the family of collectible bubble powers. Distinct from the Skills
tree, Bloom sparkle and system shutdown symbol.

Original geometric artwork created for Ricochet Bloom by Cure Interactive.
No external source artwork copied. Uses the game's 48-unit canvas, rounded
3.6-unit strokes and open negative space for small monochrome UI masks.

Used by the Powers category, Bubble Powers panel Powers Unlocked statistic and Wiki power topics.
