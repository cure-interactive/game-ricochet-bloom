# Power-up artwork

Original SVG artwork created for Ricochet Bloom by Cure Interactive. These game-specific drawings use the bold silhouettes, rounded geometry and open negative space of the approved Cure Interactive interface collection as a style reference. No external source artwork is copied. The 48-unit drawings are designed to stay distinct at 16, 24 and 48 pixels.
