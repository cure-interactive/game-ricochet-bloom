# Medals

Every scoring gamemode has **Bronze**, **Silver** and **Gold** conditions. Casual is a casual pool without medals or rewards. Open a gamemode or level to see its exact conditions before playing. The medal icons have one, two and three engraved bars, as well as different colours.

## Earning and Keeping Medals

A higher medal includes the lower tiers. Finite challenges must finish their objective; a failed or unfinished attempt earns no medal, even if a score target was reached. The whole final shot counts. Medals do not spend or replace Points.

Your best medal never goes down after a retry. Experiment records belong to the exact difficulty, preset and settings and travel with notebook exports. Normal and Development records are separate. Earlier saves are preserved; missing historical evidence is not invented. A retained completed experiment can establish its medal when opened again.

## Melody Journey

Clear the pool goal and reach its score target. All five tracks use these targets, with separate Standard and Relaxed records:

- **Intro:** Bronze 875, Silver 1,100, Gold 1,400 score.
- **Verse:** Bronze 875, Silver 1,250, Gold 1,600 score.
- **Build:** Bronze 1,750, Silver 2,500, Gold 3,500 score.
- **Bridge:** Bronze 900, Silver 1,250, Gold 1,600 score.
- **Finale:** Bronze 1,750, Silver 2,750, Gold 4,000 score.

Track awards remain separate: Bronze for completing all five pools; Silver with at least 3 bonuses; Gold with all 5 bonuses in at most 20 shots. Shared Challenges use the same pool conditions.

## Score Attack

- **5 shots:** Bronze 1,500, Silver 3,000, Gold 5,000 score. Finish every shot.
- **10 shots:** Bronze 3,000, Silver 6,000, Gold 10,000 score. Finish every shot.
- **15 shots:** Bronze 4,500, Silver 9,000, Gold 15,000 score. Finish every shot.

## Challenge

- **Bronze:** Make 3 blooms before shots run out.
- **Silver:** Make 3 blooms in at most 8 shots.
- **Gold:** Make 3 blooms in at most 5 shots.

Each pool seed keeps a separate best medal. Finish three blooms before running out of shots; bonus shots do not change the Silver or Gold shot limits.

## Free Play

- **Bronze:** Settle a shot · 1,000 score.
- **Silver:** Settle a shot · 5,000 score.
- **Gold:** Settle a shot · 15,000 score.

Use the current pool’s score. Milestones are awarded only after a shot settles; you can keep playing after Gold. Records are separate by pool seed.

## Endless Waves

- **Bronze:** Clear this wave in 5 shots.
- **Silver:** Clear this wave in at most 4 shots.
- **Gold:** Clear this wave in at most 3 shots.

Each generated wave has its own medal, saved by wave number and pool seed. No medal is awarded for the wave that ends an attempt. Gold may require stronger researched powers on later waves.

## Experiment Levels

The following are Standard conditions. Every level requires completion. One-shot puzzles judge wall banks across the entire shot; Wrap crossings are not wall banks. Other finite trials judge shots used. Settings show the resolved conditions for Custom, Gentle and Demanding setups. Very short custom budgets use seconds in motion instead of shot efficiency; aiming, pauses and beat previews do not count. Custom settings can make a board much harder or impossible, so use Standard for the authored medal challenge.

Free Composition medals require saving a composition with 1, 3 or 5 different pitches among its last 64 notes. There is no timer or losing condition.
