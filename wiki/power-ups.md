## Attract

**Common · Flow Type**

Pull nearby bubbles toward the contact point with one gathering impulse.

**Unlock:** research Wide Ripple in Ripple, then buy this power.

Upgrade in this order:

- **Deep Attraction (Rare):** Increase useful reach by 20 percent.
- **Tight Group (Rare):** Strengthen attraction while leaving a small inner spacing gap.
- **Distant Gathering (Epic):** Keep more inward pull toward the edge without reversing the gathering direction.
- **Release (Legendary):** After the gathering phase, emit a modest outward release. This makes timing meaningful instead of holding the pool in a permanent clump. Requires three mastery uses: activate this power on a shot hitting at least three bubbles, or in a cleared Melody Journey pool.

## Blaster

**Common · Propagation Type**

Automatically aim and fire three gel shots after contact. No aiming, tapping or firing controls. Each bubble scores once per shot.

**Unlock:** research Focused Impact in Impact, then buy this power.

Upgrade in this order:

- **Extra Magazine (Rare):** Carry five shots instead of three.
- **Bank Shot (Rare):** Shots bounce once off a wall. Projectile banks do not count as player wall banks.
- **Scatter Shot (Epic):** Fire three pellets in a short fan for one ammunition. Each bubble can be struck only once per volley.
- **Bloom Reload (Legendary):** The first bloom after activation refills your magazine once this shot. Requires three mastery uses: activate this power on a shot hitting at least three bubbles, or in a cleared Melody Journey pool.
