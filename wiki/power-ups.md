## Attract

**Common · Flow Type**

Pull nearby bubbles toward the contact point with one gathering impulse.

**Unlock:** research Wide Ripple in Ripple, then buy this power.

Upgrade in this order:

- **Deep Attraction (Rare):** Increase useful reach by 20 percent.
- **Tight Group (Rare):** Stronger attraction with a soft inner spacing limit to prevent a pile of overlapping bodies. Choose one gathering shape: tight group or loose ring.
- **Loose Ring (Epic):** Gather into a ring around the contact location, leaving a navigable center. Choose one gathering shape: tight group or loose ring.
- **Release (Legendary):** After the gathering phase, emit a modest outward release. This makes timing meaningful instead of holding the pool in a permanent clump. Requires three mastery uses: activate this power on a pluck hitting at least three bubbles, or in a cleared Campaign pool.

## Blaster

**Common · Propagation Type**

Automatically aim and fire three gel shots after contact. No aiming, tapping or firing controls. Each bubble scores once per pluck.

**Unlock:** research Focused Impact in Impact, then buy this power.

Upgrade in this order:

- **Extra Magazine (Rare):** Carry five shots instead of three.
- **Bank Shot (Rare):** Shots bounce once off a wall. Projectile banks do not count as player wall banks.
- **Scatter Shot (Epic):** Fire three pellets in a short fan for one ammunition. Each bubble can be struck only once per volley.
- **Bloom Reload (Legendary):** The first bloom after activation refills your magazine once this pluck. Requires three mastery uses: activate this power on a pluck hitting at least three bubbles, or in a cleared Campaign pool.

## Bubble

**Rare · Control Type**

Retain this soft interaction at a modest shell size. There is no solid collision core to unlock away.

**Unlock:** research Strong Center in Ripple, then buy this power.

Upgrade in this order:

- **Large Bubble (Rare):** Increase shell radius by 20 percent, while respecting the pool bounds.
- **Soft Bubble (Epic):** Weaker pressure on both sides; easier to envelop a cluster. Choose one Bubble mode: Soft Bubble or Firm Bubble.
- **Firm Bubble (Epic):** Stronger outward bubble pressure and a slightly stronger player reaction; better at reorganizing the pool. Choose one Bubble mode: Firm Bubble or Soft Bubble.
- **Breathing Bubble (Legendary):** Radius gently expands and contracts by 10 percent on the pulse. Contact still counts once per bubble, and radius changes never impart an instant bounce. Requires three mastery uses: activate this power on a pluck hitting at least three bubbles, or in a cleared Campaign pool.
