# How to Play

## Pull, Release, Bloom

1. Drag anywhere in the pool to pull back your glowing player. Release to launch in the opposite direction. A longer pull gives a stronger shot.
2. Touch different jellies to collect notes. Reach **five unique jellies in one pluck** to make a bloom.
3. Use wall bounces and moving jellies to extend your chain. Each jelly counts once per pluck; hitting it again can still change your motion.

A new jelly earns 25 points multiplied by its place in the chain. Wall banks add a scoring bonus, and a bloom adds 500. The bonus objective offers an additional target during play. These pool scores are separate from the research Points earned through campaign progress.

In Campaign, follow the goal displayed above the pool and finish before you run out of plucks. The first bloom in a pool earns one extra pluck. Your shot coasts to a stop before its result is resolved.

## Contact Powers

Hit a marked jelly to trigger its power. You do not need another button. **Blaster aims and fires automatically.** Collecting a power refreshes your flight time; powered motion gradually settles afterward.

Begin with one power opportunity per pluck. A pluck that blooms earns one more for the next shot, up to three. A shot without a bloom resets the next shot to one. The markers appear together at the start of the shot.

Only powers you have unlocked are included in the random pool. Research expands that variety and strengthens their effects. See the [Power-Ups reference](power-ups.md) for individual behaviors.

## Build a Song

Plucks collect melodic ideas; blooms add harmony and musical layers. Jelly markings identify **melody**, **bass** and **rhythm**. Hits on those jellies influence their part as it becomes available.

Three different jellies establish a recurring melody. Later notes develop the music over time rather than replacing it after every collision. A short answering phrase plays after a shot, and the arrangement continues between plucks. New musical layers join on the beat.

Open the music panel during play to change the instrument palette. Use Settings to balance the ambience against contact sounds.

## Controls

- Touch or mouse: drag and release in the pool.
- Keyboard: left/right arrows aim, up/down arrows change shot strength, Space plucks, and P pauses.
- Optional buttons: Settings → Accessibility → Show on-screen controls.

The main menu and its menus share a living jelly background. Press an empty area for a pulse; hold or move one or more fingers to keep pushing nearby jellies away. Menu controls and scrolling still work normally. This background does not affect your run or progress.
