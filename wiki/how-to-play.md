# How to Play

## Pull, Release, Chain

1. Drag anywhere in the pool to pull back your glowing player. Release to launch in the opposite direction. A longer pull gives a stronger shot.
2. Touch different bubbles to build a chain. Reach **five unique bubbles in one shot** to make a 5-Chain.
3. Use wall bounces and moving bubbles to extend your chain. Each bubble counts once per shot; hitting it again can still change your motion.

The initial **Drag back and release to launch** instruction stays until you successfully launch your first shot. Waiting, opening a menu, or cancelling a drag does not dismiss it.

A new bubble earns 25 points multiplied by its place in the chain. Wall banks add a scoring bonus, and a 5-Chain adds 500. The bonus objective offers an additional target during play. Score is separate from the persistent Points spent in Skills.

In Melody Journey, follow the goal displayed above the pool and finish before you run out of shots. The first 5-Chain in a pool earns one extra shot. Your shot coasts to a stop before its result is resolved.

Choose **Gamemodes → Endless Waves** from the main menu for five shots per wave: bubbles pop on their first hit, and clearing every bubble advances to a harder wave. See [Endless Waves](waves.md).

## Casual

Casual is a casual, self-playing pool with the same enlarged bubbles, player and power effects as the main-menu background. Tap or hold to stir the pool. Every five hits brings a power from your unlocked powers, with its icon shown inside a bubble. The music is the pool's composed ambient arrangement.

The only play control is the floating **Pause** button, placed in the bottom-right corner, or bottom-left with Left-Handed Layout. Pause freezes the pool while its ambient music continues; Resume continues play. Press P or Escape to pause with a keyboard. Casual uses the standard pause menu: Resume game, Main Menu, Wiki, How to Play and Settings. The pool stays paused while you read help or change settings. Returning after switching to another app or tab shows this full pause menu; play resumes only when you choose Resume.

Casual earns **no Points, medals, mastery or campaign progress**. It never replaces your saved game. There is no score target, shot limit or loss condition.

## Points

Every scoring gamemode and playable experiment preset earns permanent **Points** for Skills. Points bank when the shot settles, including a failed final shot:

- **Melody Journey, Free Play, Endless Waves, Score Attack and Challenge: +5 Points per productive shot.** Touch at least one bubble or complete the goal. More contacts, 5-Chains and cleared waves do not stack extra base Points.
- **Reaction Puzzle, Cargo Rescue and Outbreak Containment: +3 Points per productive shot.** Their shorter shots use a smaller award.
- **Trick Shots, Melody Trials and Capture Transport Release: +4 Points per productive shot.**
- **Bonus challenges: +1 Point per difficulty level.** The current challenges pay 1–3 Points. They bank once at rest. Campaign pool and track clears do not add another currency payout.

There is no timed payout, cooldown or purchase limit: misses and waiting earn nothing, and better play can earn faster. Different skills have different costs. Existing balances and owned upgrades are preserved; new prices apply to future purchases.

Every scoring game mode displays your available **Points** balance during play, matching Skills for your current profile. Experiment modes also show Points earned in that run separately. The balance updates when rewards are saved. A short message shows Points banked and your available balance. Your pool's Score remains separate. Reopening a save does not pay twice. Casual, listening, menu animation and missed shots do not earn Points. Development play pays only the separate Development profile. Shared challenges pay play Points to the profile you entered from, without awarding Melody Journey track completion to that profile.

For power mastery, activate a power you own and hit at least **three different bubbles in that shot**. Each qualifying shot earns one mastery use; successful Melody Journey pools also count. Three uses meet a capstone's mastery requirement. You can earn every power and upgrade through Free Play or Waves; Melody Journey track and skin unlocks still come from Melody Journey completion.

## Player Stats

Open **Player → Stats** for your build, play totals, records and medals. The [Player guide](player.md) covers the viewer, Skins, Stats and Player Skills.

## Contact Powers

Hit a marked bubble to trigger its power. You do not need another button. **Blaster aims and fires automatically.** Collecting a power refreshes your flight time; powered motion gradually settles afterward.

Begin with one power opportunity per shot. A shot that 5-Chains earns one more for the next shot, up to three. A shot without a 5-Chain resets the next shot to one. The markers appear together at the start of the shot.

Only powers you have unlocked are included in the random pool. Research expands that variety and strengthens their effects. See the [Power-Ups reference](power-ups.md) for individual behaviors.

## Build a Song

Shots collect melodic ideas; 5-Chains add harmony and musical layers. Bubble markings identify **melody**, **bass** and **rhythm**. Hits on those bubbles influence their part as it becomes available.

Three different bubbles establish a recurring melody. Later notes develop the music over time rather than replacing it after every collision. A short answering phrase plays after a shot, and the arrangement continues between shots. New musical layers join on the beat.

Open **Player → Music** to choose Instruments, Soundstages and Ambience without changing your composition. In **Settings → Audio**, use Music Volume to balance the music against contact sounds.

## Controls

The compact HUD keeps score, spendable Points, shots, power slots, chain dots and the bonus reward below the pool. Tap any HUD item for an information card. Score and Points explain their different roles; shots explain the budget; chain dots show progress up to five contacts, then the exact count replaces them in the same space. The Points value carries its coin icon. The medal lists every condition, power slots explain the current powers, and the checkmarked challenge flag opens the bonus objective with progress and its Points reward. Tap outside a card, press Escape or use Close to dismiss it. Pause floats in the pool corner and follows Left-Handed Layout.

- Touch or mouse: drag and release in the pool.
- Keyboard: left/right arrows aim, up/down arrows change shot strength, Space launches, and P pauses.
- Optional buttons: Settings → Accessibility → Show on-screen controls.

The pause menu identifies your current mode, track or pool and remaining goal. Resume keeps the current pool.

The main menu and its menus share a living bubble background. Press an empty area for a pulse; hold or move one or more fingers to keep pushing nearby bubbles away. Menu controls and scrolling still work normally. The background does not react to taps through an opaque menu or guide. This background does not affect your run or progress.
