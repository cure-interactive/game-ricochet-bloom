# Cosmetic Rarity

Rarity describes the richness of a cosmetic's presentation, never stronger physics, easier goals, extra Points or better rewards. Higher tiers offer more layered looks and sounds, with a recognizable theme. All tiers preserve the same readable player size and important gameplay cues.

The current paid catalogue uses **Common: 60 Points**, **Rare: 120**, **Epic: 220**, **Legendary: 360**, and **Mythic: 550**. Original defaults, Dewdrop and Sprout are free; campaign skins are earned by completing tracks. Rarity is not a random drop chance. Preview an item before choosing **Unlock & Equip**. Owned items can be equipped freely, including during a saved run.

## Skins

- **Common:** a clear color and material treatment on the familiar round membrane; Sea Glass uses translucent bands.
- **Rare:** a recognizable interior motif with restrained detail; Frost Crystal carries a snow crystal.
- **Epic:** layered interior artwork with its own slow animation; Nebula carries a turning constellation.
- **Legendary:** a strong authored identity with coordinated moving parts or motion response; Solar and Cure Interactive use distinct cores and orbits.
- **Mythic:** multiple coordinated layers that respond together to movement; Spectrum combines shifting orbits and an opening star.

Skin decoration stays on the bubble. Trails and Impacts are separate equipped choices. In the skin gallery, only the equipped preview may produce its trail, from its own movement, including a gentle local nudge from scrolling. Large previews also show the equipped trail.

## Trails

- **Common:** one clean, readable wake, such as Silk.
- **Rare:** a distinct particle motif with restrained spacing and fading, such as Stardust.
- **Epic:** coordinated colored strands or layered geometry, such as Prism's braid.
- **Legendary:** an animated material that reacts to speed, with a brief launch accent; Ember sheds cooling particles.
- **Mythic:** a cohesive combination of ribbons, particles and movement response, with a launch accent and travel sound tapering over 2.5 seconds after launch and 1.5 seconds after impacts; Aurora is the current example.

Travel sounds follow movement and fade as the player slows. Gallery animations and decorative icons stay silent; use Preview Sound to audition a style.

## Impacts

- **Common:** a brief, clear shape at contact, such as Ripple Rings.
- **Rare:** a small themed burst with distinct particles, such as Spark.
- **Epic:** a coordinated expanding motif, such as Bloom Petals.
- **Legendary:** multiple phases with a clear core and outer release, such as Sunburst.
- **Mythic:** a signature sequence of coordinated phases and a short sound accent, such as Prismatic Nova's successive shockwaves.

Impacts remain local and finite. They do not change contact timing, collision size or damage. They are decorative feedback, not extra attacks.

## Instruments

- **Common:** a simple, characterful timbre with a gentle beginning and fade; Felt Keys is soft and warm.
- **Rare:** a distinctive ringing or resonant character; Crystal Chimes adds bell-like harmonics.
- **Epic:** a coordinated ensemble or closely tuned voices; Velvet Strings swells together.
- **Legendary:** a polished signature voice with a distinctive beginning and fade; Golden Celesta carries delicate struck overtones.
- **Mythic:** a layered, evolving voice with a unified identity; Aurora Choir combines airy harmonic voices.

Instruments change timbre, not melody, rhythm or percussion patterns. Richer tiers stay within the same volume controls and preserve musical clarity.

## Soundstages

- **Common:** a simple, close acoustic space; Small Room has a short reflection.
- **Rare:** a space distinguished by material and warmth; Wooden Hall softens reflections.
- **Epic:** recognizable early reflections plus a diffuse tail; Stone Chamber provides a clear spatial identity.
- **Legendary:** a larger, layered space that keeps the direct melody clear; Cathedral has a long, spacious echo.
- **Mythic:** an imagined acoustic space with a broad sense of space; Celestial Vault unfolds across a broad tail.

Soundstages change music only; gameplay sound effects keep their familiar sound. Larger spaces are not automatically better or louder. Original retains the familiar room.

## Ambience

- **Common:** one quiet, even background texture; Soft Rain.
- **Rare:** a texture with recognizable slow motion; Quiet Shore's waves arrive and recede.
- **Epic:** a setting with complementary background detail; Night Forest combines soft air with rhythmic insect chirps and pauses between calling bouts.
- **Legendary:** coordinated layers and small material events; Ember Hearth combines low warmth, air and soft crackles.
- **Mythic:** a distinct imagined atmosphere with evolving layers; Orbital Drift blends smooth, slowly changing tones.

Ambience loops without restarting at musical phrases. It remains behind the composition, follows Music and Master volume, and stops when music is inactive or muted. **None** removes it. Ambience, Soundstage and Instrument selections are independent, so any rarity can be mixed with any other.

See [Player Music](player.md#music) for previews, purchases and saved-song behavior.

## Pendants and Companions

Pendants float passively on short, loose bungee tethers and gently avoid the player without pushing it. Companions wander on longer invisible tethers, changing direction on their own and occasionally perking up toward nearby bubbles. Both tethers pull only when stretched; neither changes player physics. Companions have no visible cord and smoothly recover their upright screen orientation. Each category has two designs per rarity, plus None. Common starters Dewdrop and Sprout are free; the second Common costs 40 Points. Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic designs cost 120, 220, 360 and 550 Points.

- **Common:** Pendants — Dewdrop, River Pebble. Companions — Sprout, Puff. Simple shapes and bounce reactions.
- **Rare:** Pendants — Silverleaf, Pearl Shell. Companions — Firefly, Dew Beetle. More detailed silhouettes and materials.
- **Epic:** Pendants — Moonstone, Astral Key. Companions — Moon Jelly, Cloud Fox. Pendants have static braided cords. Halos and a brief success pulse; companions add a delayed two-note voice.
- **Legendary:** Pendants — Sundial, Orbit Compass. Companions — Star Moth, Sunbird. Moving orbital detail, brighter pulses and three-note companion calls.
- **Mythic:** Pendants — Prism Heart, Worldseed. Companions — Bloom Dragon, Comet Whale. Pendants have animated lights flowing along their cords. Larger celebration pulses, finite star or petal bursts and layered companion voices.

5-Chains, bonus objectives, wave clears and victories trigger celebrations. Companions answer about a fifth of a second later, with a cooldown to keep nearby successes from piling up. Their short voices respect master volume, mute and pause. Pendants remain silent. Preview Celebration demonstrates the visual reaction; Preview Sound explicitly auditions eligible companions. Gallery animation and menu autoplay never play companion calls. Low quality uses fewer particles. None of these effects changes gameplay.
