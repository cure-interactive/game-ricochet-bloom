# Cosmetic Rarity

Rarity describes the richness of a cosmetic's presentation, never stronger physics, easier goals, extra Points or better rewards. Higher tiers need a coherent identity, not simply more particles, larger artwork or louder sound. All tiers preserve the same readable player size and important gameplay cues.

The current paid catalogue uses **Common: 60 Points**, **Rare: 120**, **Epic: 220**, **Legendary: 360**, and **Mythic: 550**. Original defaults are free; campaign skins are earned by completing tracks. Rarity is not a random drop chance. Preview an item before choosing **Unlock & Equip**. Owned items can be equipped freely, including during a saved run.

## Skins

- **Common:** a clear color and material treatment on the familiar round membrane; Sea Glass uses translucent bands.
- **Rare:** a recognizable interior motif with restrained detail; Frost Crystal carries a snow crystal.
- **Epic:** layered interior artwork with its own slow animation; Nebula carries a turning constellation.
- **Legendary:** a strong authored identity with coordinated moving parts or motion response; Solar and Cure Interactive use distinct cores and orbits.
- **Mythic:** multiple coordinated layers that respond together to movement; Spectrum combines shifting orbits and an opening star.

Skin decoration stays on the bubble. Trails and Impacts are separate equipped choices. In the skin gallery, only the equipped preview may produce its trail, from its own movement, including a gentle local nudge from scrolling. Page displacement itself is never drawn into the trail. Large previews also show the equipped trail.

## Trails

- **Common:** one clean, readable wake, such as Silk.
- **Rare:** a distinct particle motif with restrained spacing and fading, such as Stardust.
- **Epic:** coordinated colored strands or layered geometry, such as Prism's braid.
- **Legendary:** an animated material that reacts to speed, with a brief launch accent; Ember sheds cooling particles.
- **Mythic:** a cohesive combination of ribbons, particles and movement response, with a launch accent and continuous ambient travel sound; Aurora is the current example.

Travel audio flows continuously rather than repeating plucks. All trails follow actual movement through one trail simulation; they never compete with a second emitter. Decorative icons remain silent, and their live drawing bounds include the entire fading tail.

## Impacts

- **Common:** a brief, clear shape at contact, such as Ripple Rings.
- **Rare:** a small themed burst with distinct particles, such as Spark.
- **Epic:** a coordinated expanding motif, such as Bloom Petals.
- **Legendary:** multiple phases with a clear core and outer release, such as Sunburst.
- **Mythic:** a signature sequence of coordinated phases and a short sound accent, such as Prismatic Nova's successive shockwaves.

Impacts remain local and finite. They do not change contact timing, collision size or damage. Rarity never justifies clipping a preview or hiding the pool behind effects.

## Instruments

- **Common:** a simple, characterful timbre with a restrained envelope; Felt Keys is soft and warm.
- **Rare:** a clear new overtone identity; Crystal Chimes adds bell-like harmonics.
- **Epic:** a coordinated ensemble or closely tuned voices; Velvet Strings swells together.
- **Legendary:** a polished signature voice with carefully shaped attack and decay; Golden Celesta carries delicate struck overtones.
- **Mythic:** a layered, evolving voice with a unified identity; Aurora Choir combines airy harmonic voices.

Instruments change timbre, not melody, rhythm or percussion patterns. They use the local synthesis engine, with no sampled SoundFont downloads. Richer tiers stay within the same volume controls and preserve musical clarity.

## Soundstages

- **Common:** a simple, close acoustic space; Small Room has a short reflection.
- **Rare:** a space distinguished by material and warmth; Wooden Hall softens reflections.
- **Epic:** recognizable early reflections plus a diffuse tail; Stone Chamber provides a clear spatial identity.
- **Legendary:** a larger, layered space that keeps the direct melody clear; Cathedral has a tall, finite tail.
- **Mythic:** an imagined acoustic space with coordinated stereo diffusion; Celestial Vault unfolds across a broad tail.

Soundstages process music only. Their reverberation is finite, with no accumulating feedback. Larger spaces are not automatically better or louder. Original retains the familiar room.

## Ambience

- **Common:** one quiet, even background texture; Soft Rain.
- **Rare:** a texture with recognizable slow motion; Quiet Shore's waves arrive and recede.
- **Epic:** a setting with complementary background detail; Night Forest combines air and insect tones.
- **Legendary:** coordinated layers and small material events; Ember Hearth combines low warmth, air and soft crackles.
- **Mythic:** a distinct imagined atmosphere with evolving layers; Orbital Drift combines breathing air and harmonic light.

Ambience loops without restarting at musical phrases. It remains behind the composition, follows Music and Master volume, and stops when music is inactive or muted. **None** removes it. Ambience, Soundstage and Instrument selections are independent, so any rarity can be mixed with any other.
