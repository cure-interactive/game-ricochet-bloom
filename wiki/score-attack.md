# Score Attack

Choose **Gamemodes → Score Attack**. Pick 5, 10 or 15 shots; 10 is selected initially. Chase a personal best with the same physics, chains, banks and blooms used in Melody Journey.

## Every Shot Counts

There is no countdown. Bubbles drift while you aim. Blooms give score and Points but no extra shots. Three blooms do not end the run. Your last shot completes its entire flight and settles before the result is recorded.

Each new run begins with the same pool layout. Your owned skills and active choices are fixed at the start of the attempt. Purchases remain available in the Skill Tree and apply to the next attempt.

## Score and Medals

Each different bubble touched during a shot adds 25 times its position in the chain: 25, 50, 75 and so on. A new contact after a wall bank adds another 25. Five unique contacts trigger a bloom worth 500 more. Repeat contacts with the same bubble do not extend that shot's chain.

- **5 shots:** Bronze 1,500, Silver 3,000, Gold 5,000.
- **10 shots:** Bronze 3,000, Silver 6,000, Gold 10,000.
- **15 shots:** Bronze 4,500, Silver 9,000, Gold 15,000.

Targets are inclusive. Finish every shot to record a best, even if your score is below Bronze. A medal requires reaching its score target. The HUD shows progress toward all three targets throughout the run. These are initial balance values; real simulation verifies Gold is possible with starter Ripple in every budget.

## Points and Saves

Score measures performance; **Points** are the spendable Skill Tree currency. The usual contact, bloom and difficulty-scaled bonus rewards apply, including power mastery. Score itself is not converted into Points, and simply finishing or restarting grants no extra payout.

Every budget keeps a separate resumable run and completed personal best. Normal and Development profiles are separate. Reloading can resume a flight without paying twice. Restarting replaces that budget's current run while preserving its completed best and earned Points. An unreadable save is preserved until you explicitly replace it; an unreadable best cannot be recovered by replacement.

These are personal records across your skill progression, not equal-loadout competitive rankings. Music is still generated, while its panel follows the Development disclosure outside Melody Journey.
