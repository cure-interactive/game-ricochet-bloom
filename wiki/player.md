# Player

Open **Player** from the main menu to view your equipped skin and choose Skins, Stats or Skills.

## Player Viewer

The large preview shows your equipped skin. Drag it to stretch its tether, or tap it to make it bounce. Preview interactions are cosmetic and do not change a run.

## Skins

Open **Player → Skins** to browse appearances. Original is available from the start; completing a Melody Journey track unlocks its skin. Select a skin to inspect its large preview and unlock information, then equip an owned skin. Skins change appearance, not gameplay strength.

## Stats

Open **Player → Stats** to see your researched launch speed, aiming-guide lengths, wall speed retention, Points, upgrades and recorded play totals. Overview, Records and Medals group your information, with links to Player Skills and Power Skills.

The screen also shows Melody Journey clears, separate 5/10/15-shot Score Attack bests, experiment results and Bronze/Silver/Gold records by gamemode. Standard and Relaxed campaign records stay separate. Each saved record counts only its best medal.

Stats belong to the selected Normal or Development profile in this browser. Older play may not have been recorded, and unreadable records appear as unavailable. Development grants and unlocks are not counted as earned Points or completed tracks. The displayed attributes describe your researched build; active runs and fixed challenges can retain a different build.

## Skills

Open **Player → Skills** for the Sight, Launch and Rebound branches. You can also switch between Player and Powers inside Skills. Both trees spend the same Points wallet; purchases are permanent and cumulative.

See [Player Upgrades](skill-tree.md#player-upgrades) for effects, costs and where your researched build applies. [Medals](medals.md) explains the conditions behind your records.
