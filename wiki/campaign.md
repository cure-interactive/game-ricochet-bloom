# Campaign

## Choose a Track

Campaign shows the tracks you have unlocked. Select a track to open its own page with goals, rewards and a start or continue action. Locked tracks show what must be completed first.

The sequence is **First Light → Crosscurrent → Glasswater → Afterimage → Night Bloom**. Completing a track opens the next one and unlocks its player skin. Choose an earned skin from Player Skins on the main menu.

## Five Pools per Run

Each track follows the same five-pool structure with its own atmosphere and arrangement:

1. **Intro:** make one bloom, with 12 jellies.
2. **Verse:** make a bloom and touch all three musical roles, with 14 jellies.
3. **Build:** make two blooms, with 16 jellies.
4. **Bridge:** make a bloom with a wall bank, with 14 jellies.
5. **Finale:** make two blooms and touch all three roles, with 18 jellies.

New runs give six plucks per pool. The first bloom adds one more. Your song develops across the pools; cleared pools lead to an interlude before you continue.

## Research Points and Discoveries

Clearing a pool earns research Points. Completing its bonus and finishing a track provide further rewards, including a first-clear reward. Spend research Points in the [Skill Tree](skill-tree.md). Your large in-pool score is a different measure; it is not your research balance.

Use a power in a pool you successfully clear to build its mastery toward an Epic upgrade. Some rewards can also provide a temporary discovery during a run; that does not permanently purchase its research prerequisite.

## Continue or Start Again

The main menu's Continue button names your current track and pool. Pausing or returning to the menu preserves the run. Ending an active run from its track page asks for confirmation.

Free Play is a separate main-menu choice with unlimited plucks and the powers you have unlocked. Use it to enjoy the pool and its music without campaign pool goals.

Saved songs are available within Campaign. Browser saves belong to this device and site; see [Saves](settings.md#saves-and-backups) before moving to another device.
