# Play the Experiments

Open **Main Menu → Gamemodes** to choose Melody Journey, Free Play, Endless Waves, Challenge or one of three experiments. Each mode has its own illustrated card. Each experiment offers three layouts and a separate Continue button when a trial is saved.

## Controls and Saves

The board waits while you aim. Drag backward anywhere on the board and release to launch, or use the Aim and Power sliders followed by Launch. With the board focused, Left/Right adjust direction, Up/Down adjust power and Space launches. Escape pauses. There is no countdown.

A shot resolves after all motion settles. You can pause during motion and continue the same shot after reopening the game. Modes returns to the layout menu. Starting another layout replaces only that experiment's saved trial. Retry Layout starts the same arrangement after a win or loss.

Each experiment saves its board and generated song separately from the original modes and from the other experiments. Normal and Development profiles also have separate trial saves. Trial boards currently remain local to this browser and are not included in the legacy backup export. Avoid clearing browser data if you want to keep them.

## Reaction Puzzle

Clear eight hazards in six shots while preserving all three healthy cells. Pink circles are targets, orange bursts are blast bubbles, flame symbols are fire and green bubbles contain gas. Crosses mark protected cells. Damaging a protected cell loses the trial, even if the same shot clears every hazard.

Touching a blast bubble triggers its blast and can chain through nearby blast or gas bubbles. Fire ignites nearby gas. Touching gas directly vents it safely. Solid walls block blasts as well as providing bank shots; faint rings show blast reach. Try **First Spark**, **Firebreak** and **Chain of Islands**.

## Cargo Rescue

Deliver two of three blue cargo bubbles into the mint receiver within eight shots. Contact pushes cargo. Spikes and blasts rupture it; losing two cargo bubbles ends the trial. Cargo already delivered is safe.

Arrows show a current that pushes cargo toward the receiver early in each shot. Striped barriers can be destroyed by a blast, but nearby cargo is vulnerable too. Other walls remain solid. Try **Open Water**, **Narrow Passage** and **The Long Way**.

## Outbreak Containment

Seal both vents and remove all infected motes within eight shots. Push diamond-shaped plugs into the ringed vents. The central healthy cell starts with three integrity points; losing all three ends the trial.

After an unfinished shot settles, existing infected motes advance toward the cell, then each open vent emits one new mote, up to six active motes. A mote that reaches the cell consumes itself and removes one integrity point. The vent label shows when production is capped. No extra outbreak turn follows a completed objective or the final shot.

Contact clears infected motes. Blasts clear nearby motes and can push plugs. Popping the brown mud bubble creates a slowing area for infected movement. Try **First Response**, **Crossfire** and **Quarantine**. These are invented cellular rules, not a biological simulation.

## Points and Music

All three experiments earn persistent Skill Tree Points: one per six unique contacts accumulated across the trial, one for a chain of at least five contacts in a shot, and three for completing the objective. Rewards bank when the shot settles, including losses. Resuming the same receipt does not pay twice.

Trials use fixed environmental tools so existing research does not change the starting puzzle. They do not train permanent power mastery; the original modes still do. Contact-generated music accompanies play, while the visible goals and reaction log explain outcomes with sound muted.

## What to Test

All nine layouts have verified solutions through the simulation. Balance, readability, touch comfort and replay interest still need human playtesting. Predict one interaction before launching, then compare what actually happened. Try a different route and see whether you can reproduce a useful chain deliberately.

The [Game Modes to Try](game-modes.md) page keeps the design questions and reserve ideas. The [Development Concept](development-concept.md) remains the broader workshop; acid, steam merging, shrapnel and a full cellular campaign are future possibilities.
