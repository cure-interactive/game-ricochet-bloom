# Sound, Settings & Saves

Pause offers Resume Game, Main Menu, Wiki, How to Play and Settings. Open Skill Tree and Player Skins from the Main Menu.

## Audio

Settings → Audio contains the sound toggle, master volume and ambience volume. Both volume sliders start at 100%. Lower ambience to hear the contact and jelly sounds more prominently. Sound Check plays a short cue at your current sound and volume settings.

Audio begins after an interaction with the game. Leaving the page pauses the game and its sound; use Resume when you return. The music panel offers Glass, Velvet and Wood instrument palettes.

## Accessibility and Display

An available unchecked setting has a bright solid outline; checked settings are mint. Disabled unchecked controls have a muted dashed outline. A disabled checked control retains its gray check, so its stored selection stays clear. Disabled controls cannot be changed until their requirement is met.

Show on-screen controls adds shot-control buttons and skill-tree camera controls. It is off by default; touch gestures and keyboard controls remain available.

Gentle Visuals reduces glow and motion intensity and is off by default. Stream View offers a wider pool layout on larger screens. Screen Mode explains the fullscreen or app-view options available on your current device; browser support differs.

## Saves and Backups

Your pool, song and campaign progress save in this browser. They do not automatically transfer between devices or between the local, GitHub Pages and private hosted copies. Clearing website data can remove them.

Use the backup export and import controls in Settings → Advanced & About → Development & Backups to keep or transfer your progress. Keep a backup before replacing browser data.

Development controls use a separate test profile. They can reset test progress, maximize campaign access or maximize the skill tree without awarding normal campaign receipts. **Show Hidden Tracks, Skills and Skins** reveals locked tracks, research and player skins for inspection in either profile without changing unlocks or progress. Your previous Show All Skills preference carries over to this combined setting. **Show Purchased Skill Costs** displays catalogue prices on owned skill nodes and cards. Both view toggles are off by default. These toggles, Development mode and the Points-to-add amount are remembered in this browser, including after reopening the game. Test unlocks, Points and runs also remain saved; reloading does not repeat grants or maximize actions. Switch Development mode off to return to your normal progress. Clearing browser data clears these preferences, and other browsers or devices keep their own settings.

## Screen Rotation

The game supports portrait and landscape. Rotate your device to change the layout; browser toolbar changes do not rotate the pool. If Safari stays upright on iPhone, turn off Portrait Orientation Lock in Control Center. Safari’s Page Menu beside the address field belongs to the browser, not the game.
