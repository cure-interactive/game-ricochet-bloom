# Sound, Settings & Saves

The full-screen pause menu shares the main menu’s bubble background, game title and version. It offers Resume Game, Main Menu, Wiki, How to Play and Settings. Open Skills and Player Skins from the Main Menu. Settings → About displays the installed game version, Cure Interactive logo and game information, with access to Icon Credits and [Change Logs](changelog.md).

## Audio

Settings → Audio contains **Sound**, **Master Volume** and **Music Volume**. Both volume sliders start at 100%. Drag the thumb or tap the track; keyboard users can focus a slider and use arrow keys, Home or End. Sliders, dragged tab selectors and the left-edge Back gesture give short clicks that rise with forward movement and fall when you reverse. They follow Sound and Master Volume. Lower Music Volume to hear contact and bubble sounds more prominently. **Sound Check** plays three gameplay notes at low, medium and full strength, following Master Volume. Press it again to restart the check. It works while paused, is unaffected by Music Volume, and does not change your song. Overlapping gameplay sounds can still make the mix louder than a single note.

When you return after switching away or reloading, **Welcome Back** lets you choose **Continue** to restore sound and return to your saved page. Gameplay remains paused until you choose Resume. The welcome screen is skipped when Sound is off or Master Volume is zero. If sound cannot restart, you can try Continue again or continue without sound. Leaving the page quickly fades sound to silence and pauses play. Your Sound and volume choices still apply. The pause menu freezes play while ambient music continues; an already-open settings or help page stays where you left it. Open **Player → Music** to choose and preview Instruments, Soundstages and Ambience.

Buttons, navigation and settings changes play short UI sounds, including while paused. These use Sound and Master Volume; Music Volume does not change them. Turning Sound off also silences UI sounds.

The main-menu background is a self-playing Free Play pool using your unlocked powers, player upgrades and equipped skin. It plays its musical arrangement, without collision or power sound effects. Sound and Music Volume control it too. Menu play does not earn Points or change your saved game or song.

Dragging sliders or tab selectors plays the lowest original tone at every position. Edge-pull Back keeps its four rising and falling tones. Feedback occurs at each value increment or eight pixels of travel, whichever comes first. Scrolling a tab row without dragging its selector stays silent.

## Accessibility and Display

An available unchecked setting has a bright solid outline; checked settings are mint. Disabled unchecked controls have a muted dashed outline. A disabled checked control retains its gray check, so its stored selection stays clear. Disabled controls cannot be changed until their requirement is met. Settings pair a descriptive icon and label with a checkbox on the right. Single-choice menus such as Rendering Quality use radio indicators; choosing an option closes the menu.

Show on-screen controls adds shot-control buttons and skill-tree camera controls. It is off by default; touch gestures and keyboard controls remain available.

Rendering Quality offers High, Balanced and Low. High keeps full detail, Balanced softens fine detail, and Low also reduces decorative effects. All levels keep bubble deformation, player jiggle and interaction active. Previous Gentle Visuals preferences become Low quality; the operating system’s reduced-motion preference does not override game animation. Frame Rate offers **Device default**, **60 FPS** and **30 FPS** as maximum drawing rates; gameplay speed stays the same. **Automatic Battery Saver** selects Low quality and 30 FPS at 20% battery or below while unplugged, where the browser provides usable battery information. Turn it off to keep your choices. Browsers cannot reliably report the operating system’s Low Power Mode; manual Low/30 FPS remains available. **Device Motion** lets supported previews react to tilt and movement, with an explicit permission action where required. Screen Mode explains the fullscreen or app-view options available on your current device; browser support differs. Installation steps use platform-specific icons and bold action names. **Settings → Updates → Automatic updates** is on by default in browsers and installed apps. It checks when the game opens, gains focus or returns to view. An available update restarts the game only after progress saves successfully. Turn it off to check manually with **Check for Updates**. Your choice persists; offline or failed checks retry on a later return.

When opening the game, the loading screen stays in place while your saved page is restored and automatic updates are checked. It fades away over the ready page; the main menu has no separate entrance animation.

## Left-Handed Layout

Back controls sit on the right by default. Enable **Settings → Accessibility → Left-Handed Layout** to place Back, Close, Pause, favorite stars and Skills legend on the left. The setting is remembered on this browser and applies to both Normal and Development profiles. The Points/selector arrangement and physical tab order mirror too. Text, aiming and the left-edge Back gesture keep their normal direction. Skills and Stats selectors support both tapping a label and dragging the selection; the highlight stays inside the control. Scroll content normally, or grab the visible scrollbar thumb to drag and fling it.

## Copy Diagnostics

Use **Settings → Get Help → Copy Report** for one complete support report. If the game cannot start or loading takes longer than expected, **Copy Diagnostics** remains available on the startup screen. That fallback includes the game version, browser, screen size, loading state, recent errors and read-only save metadata, without saved-game contents. Copying never changes progress; if clipboard access fails, selected report text lets you copy manually.

## Getting Help

Open **Settings** and tap **Get Help** at the bottom, beneath About. **Email Report** opens an email to **main@cureinteractive.com** with the subject **Ricochet Bloom — Support Report — your player name** and your device details, diagnostics and game progress filled in; tap Send in your email app. The report includes your player name. **Share Report** opens your device’s sharing options; **Copy Report** lets you paste the same text into a message. No downloaded file is needed. All three actions use the same complete report, including technical diagnostics, audio state, rendering settings, recent errors and progress. There is only one copy action in Get Help.

If saving fails, the recovery panel offers these same actions and **Retry Save**. Keep the tab open so pending progress remains available. Do not clear browser data to troubleshoot.

## Saves and Backups

Your pool, song and campaign progress save in this browser. They do not automatically transfer between devices or between the local, GitHub Pages and private hosted copies. Clearing website data can remove them.

Use the backup export and import controls in Settings → Advanced → Development & Backups to keep or transfer your progress. Keep a backup before replacing browser data.

Normal and Development use separate progress. Switching back to Normal restores your normal profile; test purchases and runs do not overwrite it. View preferences such as **Show Hidden Tracks, Skills and Skins** and **Show purchased items cost** do not grant items or change purchase prices. Reloading keeps your choices; another browser or device has its own settings.

## Screen Rotation

The game supports portrait and landscape. Rotate your device to change the layout; browser toolbar changes do not rotate the pool. If Safari stays upright on iPhone, turn off Portrait Orientation Lock in Control Center. Safari’s Page Menu beside the address field belongs to the browser, not the game.
