# Sound, Settings & Saves

## Audio

Settings → Audio contains the sound toggle, master volume and ambience volume. Both volume sliders start at 100%. Lower ambience to hear the contact and jelly sounds more prominently. Sound Check plays a short cue at your current sound and volume settings.

Audio begins after an interaction with the game. Leaving the page pauses the game and its sound; use Resume when you return. The music panel offers Glass, Velvet and Wood instrument palettes.

## Accessibility and Display

Show on-screen controls adds shot-control buttons and skill-tree camera controls. It is off by default; touch gestures and keyboard controls remain available.

Gentle Visuals reduces glow and motion intensity and is off by default. Stream View offers a wider pool layout on larger screens. Screen Mode explains the fullscreen or app-view options available on your current device; browser support differs.

## Saves and Backups

Your pool, song and campaign progress save in this browser. They do not automatically transfer between devices or between the local, GitHub Pages and private hosted copies. Clearing website data can remove them.

Use the backup export and import controls in Settings → Advanced & About → Development & Backups to keep or transfer your progress. Keep a backup before replacing browser data.

Development controls use a separate test profile. They can reset test progress, maximize campaign access or maximize the skill tree without awarding normal campaign receipts. **Show All Skills** reveals locked research for inspection in either profile without changing purchases or progress. It is off by default. This toggle, Development mode and the Points-to-add amount are remembered in this browser, including after reopening the game. Test unlocks, Points and runs also remain saved; reloading does not repeat grants or maximize actions. Switch Development mode off to return to your normal progress. Clearing browser data clears these preferences, and other browsers or devices keep their own settings.
